# Market Sizing Methodology Deep Dive

This is a workflow aid. It supplies no market facts or default market-share assumptions.

## 1. Define scope

Record the product, customer, use case, geography, currency, reference period, and whether the measure is annual revenue, transaction value, users, or customer accounts. Avoid mixing GMV with provider revenue or users with paying accounts.

## 2. Bottom-up

For each non-overlapping segment, estimate eligible customers and annual revenue per customer from dated, traceable evidence.

`TAM = sum(segment eligible customers × annual revenue per customer)`

`SAM = sum(customers satisfying all explicit serviceability filters × annual revenue per customer)`

Document segment definitions, deduplication, source coverage, and uncertainty ranges. Use sensitivity cases where inputs are uncertain.

## 3. Top-down

Use a source only when its market definition, geography, period, unit, and method fit the question. Apply filters only when the filtered population or share has evidence. Do not multiply a broad category estimate by unexplained percentages.

## 4. Value theory

Quantify the customer problem, measured solution impact, and observed or tested willingness to pay. Keep stated preference, observed purchase, and inferred price separate. Present ranges and assumptions; do not prescribe a universal percentage of created value.

## 5. Obtainable market

Build SOM from reachable customers and revenue over an explicit horizon. Model qualified leads, conversion, sales capacity, retention, delivery constraints, and competition. Cap customer counts by serviceable demand and operating capacity.

## 6. Triangulation and evidence

Compare methods only after aligning definitions and periods. Explain gaps; do not impose a fixed acceptable percentage difference. For every material input record origin/URL, publication date, reference period, unit, geography, definition, access state, and whether it is observed, user-provided, assumed, or calculated. Do not fabricate source details. If access fails, mark the item unverified.
