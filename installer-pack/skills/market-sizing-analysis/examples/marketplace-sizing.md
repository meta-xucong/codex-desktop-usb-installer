# Hypothetical Marketplace Sizing Example

> **Synthetic teaching example.** Numbers below are invented solely to demonstrate formulas. They are not market data or benchmarks.

Assume a hypothetical marketplace category has 2,000 potential annual transactions, with $500 average transaction value. Assume, for illustration only, a 10% platform take rate.

- Hypothetical category GMV = 2,000 × $500 = $1,000,000/year
- Hypothetical platform revenue TAM = $1,000,000 × 10% = $100,000/year

Assume 600 transactions meet a stated serviceability filter:

- Hypothetical serviceable GMV = 600 × $500 = $300,000/year
- Hypothetical SAM platform revenue = $300,000 × 10% = $30,000/year

For an explicitly synthetic operating scenario, assume the team can facilitate 120 transactions in Year 3 and 240 in Year 5:

- Year 3 SOM revenue = 120 × $500 × 10% = $6,000
- Year 5 SOM revenue = 240 × $500 × 10% = $12,000

Real analyses must source eligible transaction counts, average values, take rates, and serviceability filters; they must also model buyer/seller liquidity, repeat rates, and operational capacity. GMV is not platform revenue.
